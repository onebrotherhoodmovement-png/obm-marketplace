Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

OBM Exchange (obmexchange.com) is a community marketplace run by One Brotherhood Movement. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it. Questions? Contact us at onebrotherhoodmovement@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address and sign-in details when you create an account.
  • Listings you post: title, price, category, area, description, and photos.
  • Messages: messages you send to buyers or sellers through the app.
  • Reports and safety messages you send us.
  • Basic technical information, such as browser type and IP address, which our service providers record in their normal server logs.
  • On your own device: your saved listings and which messages you have read are stored in your browser or app, not on our servers.

How we use it

We use this information to run the marketplace: showing listings, delivering messages, keeping the community safe, responding to reports, and fixing problems.

Who can see it

  • Listings and their photos are public. Anyone who visits the site can see them.
  • Messages are visible to the two people in the conversation. We may review them only if needed to investigate abuse, enforce our Terms, or follow the law.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We use Supabase to store accounts, listings, messages, and photos, and GitHub to host the website. They handle data on our behalf under their own security and privacy practices.

Payments

OBM Exchange does not process payments. Buyers and sellers meet in person and arrange payment themselves. We never collect card or bank details.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your own listings and photos at any time inside the app. To delete your account and the information tied to it, email onebrotherhoodmovement@gmail.com and we will remove it within 30 days, except where we must keep something to follow the law or resolve a safety issue.

Age

OBM Exchange is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, but no online service is completely secure. Use a strong password and do not share it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. If you keep using OBM Exchange after a change, you accept the updated policy.

Contact

One Brotherhood Movement, onebrotherhoodmovement@gmail.com.
