OBM Marketplace

Buy, sell, and meet up locally. Built by One Brotherhood Movement.

Post an item

No payments in the app. You and the buyer agree on a price and meet in person.

Meet up safely

  • Meet in a busy public place. Many police stations have a marked meetup spot.
  • Bring a friend, or tell someone where you are going.
  • Meet in daylight when you can.
  • Check the item before you hand over any money.
  • Never send a deposit or gift card before you meet. Real sellers don’t ask for one.
  • Report any listing or person that feels wrong.

Pay in person only. If someone asks for a deposit, Zelle, Cash App, or gift cards first, it may be a scam.
